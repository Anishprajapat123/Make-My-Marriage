## Applied direction

- Newsreader editorial headings, Plus Jakarta Sans controls, plum accent, warm
  ivory canvas, white section cards and neutral inset panels.
- Three settings sections: personal profile, wedding details with read-only
  essentials, and publication/privacy with switch controls. Separate details and
  publication saves preserve unsaved values in the other section. Profile saves
  remain independent. Open-public-page uses saved publication state only.
- Gallery album tabs, real photo-slot capacity, shareable-delivery notice, single
  upload panel, thumbnail cards with actual dimensions/size and removal controls.
  Create-album is revealed on demand. Recovery uses persisted operation states.
- Livestream visibility-requirement cards, on-demand create/edit form, two-column
  stream cards on desktop and stacked cards on mobile. Visibility labels use the
  complete server-verified publication gates. No fabricated video availability.
- Public page: monogram, centered wedding hero, functional section anchors, schedule
  cards, responsive gallery, external broadcast cards and a simple celebration footer.
  Private/unpublished sections are absent. Neutral unavailable-page presentation.

## Deliberate corrections to generated designs

The images introduce unsupported or excluded content despite the prompt. Omitted:
prototype state controls, ZIP download, album-wide archive download, fake progress
percentages/processing stages, invented photo filenames/tags, live viewer totals,
stream durations/quality claims, concierge/registry links, travel/stay, seating,
fictional subscription tiers and compliance/encryption claims. Cache removal times
are not promised. Public pages retain the existing token-based RSVP separation.

Existing workspace navigation is retained rather than introducing a competing
mobile navigation system. Livestreams use an honest external-broadcast panel rather
than generated preview photographs or an embedded player. No video-preview API or
paid provider was added. Forms open inline rather than introducing extra dialogs;
all displayed controls have real supported actions.

## Verification boundary

Source previews and HTML were inspected; this is not a claim of pixel-identical
rendering or browser acceptance. Live upload/provider mutation, browser walkthrough,
role/tenant acceptance and deployment are separate checks. No Stitch designs were
modified and no new packages were installed for alignment.
